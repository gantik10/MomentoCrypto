Common questions

GOT QUESTIONS?

What do I get when I join?

You get live signals in our private Telegram channel, with entry, take-profit and stop-loss, plus leverage details. We post updates as trades develop. A full performance dashboard is included, and everything reaches your phone through Telegram.

How many signals per day should I expect?

Expect 2–6 signals a day on average. We post high-conviction setups when our traders see a clear edge, not to fill a quota. Quality comes before quantity.

What is your win rate?

Win rate shifts with market conditions, so we don’t publish a fixed number. We’ve stayed profitable through bull and bear markets, but your results aren’t promised. Every trade is tracked in our private Telegram channel for members to review before committing longer-term.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and crypto through Whop. Checkout shows prices in your local currency, with no surprise conversion fees.

Is this a subscription? Will I be charged again?

No. You pay once for your chosen period. There’s no auto-renewal and no hidden charges. When your access ends, you decide whether to buy another plan.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Whop processes payments for thousands of online businesses. You enter your card details directly into Whop’s secure checkout. They never touch our servers.

What happens right after I pay?

Your payment success page shows a unique, one-time activation code. Open our Telegram bot and paste it there. You’ll be added to our private Telegram channel in under a minute.

Do I need trading experience?

No. Every signal includes an entry zone, take-profit targets and a hard stop-loss. If you can place a market or limit order on Binance, Bybit or any major exchange, you can follow along.

What if I miss a signal?

Turn on notifications for our private Telegram channel. Signals are time-sensitive. We pin every active trade so you can check for late entries, but review the setup rather than chase a move.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Plans are non-refundable once you use the activation code, which grants access for the full paid period. If you have an issue before activation, message support within 24 hours and we’ll help resolve it.

What coins do you trade?

Mainly BTC, ETH, SOL and top-20 altcoins by volume. We occasionally trade high-momentum meme coins, including DOGE, PEPE and WIF, when the setup is clear. No shitcoins or pump-and-dumps.
